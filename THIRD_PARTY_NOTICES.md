## Flagpedia and FlagCDN raster artwork

Flagcn loads PNG, WebP, and JPEG artwork at runtime from [Flagpedia's FlagCDN](https://flagpedia.net/download/api). U.S. state SVGs and original-proportion SVGs use FlagCDN as a fallback because Flag Icons does not provide those exact assets.

Flagpedia states that its flag images are public domain, free for commercial and non-commercial use, and based on Wikimedia Commons vector files. Flagpedia says a backlink is appreciated but not required. Flagcn keeps that backlink in its documentation and site footer.
